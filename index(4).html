[ 02 / ABOUT ]

I CUT TO
THE BEAT.

I'm Sarthak, a video editor. I started by cutting clips for fun, trying to make a random pile of footage feel like a story. Somewhere along the way it stopped being a hobby and became the thing I couldn't stop doing.

Editing made me curious about what happens before the cut, so I picked up a camera. Shooting my own footage changed how I edit: I know what a shot needs to give me, so I frame for the edit and don't have to fix it later.

Music runs through everything I make. I find the song first, and the rhythm decides where every cut, transition and sound effect lands. If a video works with the sound off, it should work even better with it on.

Today I edit for food brands, campuses, an EV platform and an agency, mostly short-form reels built to hold attention.
